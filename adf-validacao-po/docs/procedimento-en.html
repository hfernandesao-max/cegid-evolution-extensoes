Procedure – Recording Supplier Advance Payments (ADF) with the PO number

CodeTo be assignedVersion1.0Date04-11-2026
Prepared byDigital Projects ManagementApproved byHenrique FernandesApplicationCegid Evolution ERP Primavera – Contas Correntes (Accounts Payable/Receivable)

1. Purpose

To ensure that every Supplier Advance Payment (ADF) identifies the purchase order (PO) it relates to, so that advance payments can be tracked, reconciliation between Procurement, Treasury and Accounting is faster, and processing delays are avoided.

2. Scope

Applies to creating and changing ADFs for Fornecedor (Supplier) entities in the 17 group companies on Cegid Evolution ERP Primavera. It does not apply to ADFs for other entities (e.g. account 349100).

3. Definitions

  • ADF – Adiantamento a Fornecedor (Supplier Advance Payment), recorded in Contas Correntes.
  • PO – Purchase order in Primavera: types PO, POL, POS, POM, POI and ECF.
  • Número field – the ADF "Número do Documento" (Document Number), where the PO is entered.

4. Responsibilities

  • Procurement – create and approve the PO in Primavera before the advance is requested, and provide its number.
  • Treasury / Accounts Payable – record the ADF with the correct PO in the Número field.
  • Finance – review the list of ADFs without a valid PO every month.
  • Digital Projects Management – support and maintenance of the ERP validation.

5. Prerequisites

  • The PO exists in the same company, is not cancelled and belongs to the same supplier as the ADF.
  • User with access to record documents in Contas Correntes.

6. Procedure

#Action
1In Contas Correntes, open Novos Documentos (New Documents) and select the ADF document.
2In Entidade (Entity), select Fornecedor (Supplier) and enter the code (e.g. F000201).
3In the Número field, delete the number suggested by the system and type the PO(s) first, in the format TYPE SERIES/NUMBER. After the PO, any text may follow (e.g. the supplier pro forma).
4Several POs in the same ADF: separate them with a semicolon, e.g. PO 2026/10; PO 2026/11 - Kituco PP2026B/11-12.
5Fill in the dates, treasury item and amount as usual, and click Gravar (Save).
6If a warning appears, the ADF is not saved: close the warning, correct the Número field as shown in section 7 and save again.

7. Número field rule and validation messages

8. Control checks

  • Validation is automatic when new or changed ADFs are saved.
  • Finance reviews the ADFs without a valid PO list every month and requests correction of advances recorded before this procedure took effect, starting with those with an amount still to be settled.

9. Notes

  • Advance without a PO: not allowed; Procurement must create the PO first.
  • Questions or system errors: contact Digital Projects Management.
